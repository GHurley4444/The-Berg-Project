# The Party Game

A duel tournament for a whole party, played on one phone.

- **Tournament:** add everyone (2–16 players) and the app draws a bracket. Two people duel at a time.
- **Betting:** everyone else starts with 100 points. Before every mini-game, the game and its rules are revealed, then the watchers bet on who'll win it. Winners split the whole pot by stake, so backing the underdog pays more. A tied game refunds all bets. Winning a duel earns the duelist 25 points.
- **Duels:** lay the phone flat between the two duelists. Each owns one half of the screen, and the top half is flipped to face the far player. A duel is best of 1, 3 or 5 mini-games.
- **Real-life challenges:** some games (staring contest, thumb war, arm wrestle…) happen off the screen. The phone shows the rules, a timer and any secret prompt, and the crowd picks the winner.
- **Quick duel** for two people with no bets, and a **game library** to switch games on or off or try any one.

Progress is saved in the browser, so a refresh doesn't lose the tournament.

## Mini-games (53)

### Reflex (8)

| Game | How to win |
| --- | --- |
| Quick Draw | Wait for GO, then tap first. Tap early, or on a fake word, and you lose. |
| Let Go | Both press and hold. When LET GO appears, lift your finger first. Let go early and you lose. |
| Color Clash | Words flash up in colored ink. Tap only when the ink matches the word. |
| Snap | Pictures flash one after another. Tap when the same picture shows twice in a row. |
| Green Light | The light keeps changing. Tap the moment it turns green. Tapping on red or yellow loses. |
| Ping Pong | Tap to hit the ball back when it's inside your zone. Miss it and you lose. It speeds up every hit. |
| Bullseye | A marker slides back and forth. Tap to stop it as close to the middle as you can. Three shots each, best total wins. |
| Odd One Out | One picture in the grid is different. Tap it first. A wrong tap freezes you for a second. |

### Speed (7)

| Game | How to win |
| --- | --- |
| Tug of War | Mash your side to push the yellow line. Shove it all the way across to win. |
| Target Rush | Targets pop up on your side. Hit 8 of them before your opponent does. |
| Balloon Pump | Tap as fast as you can to pump up your balloon. First to pop it wins. |
| Number Hunt | Numbers 1 to 12 are scattered on your side. Tap them in order. First to 12 wins. |
| Swipe Race | An arrow appears. Swipe that way. Eight correct swipes wins. A wrong swipe freezes you briefly. |
| Whack-a-Mole | Moles pop out of the holes. Whack them for a point. Hit a bomb and lose two. First to 10. |
| Math Race | Solve the sum and tap the right answer first. Wrong answers cost a point. First to 5. |

### Memory (6)

| Game | How to win |
| --- | --- |
| Copy the Pattern | Watch the pads light up, then repeat the pattern on your side. It gets longer until someone slips. |
| Memory Grid | Some squares light up for a moment. Tap all of them from memory. One wrong square and you're out of that board. |
| What Changed? | Memorize six pictures. They hide, then come back with one swapped. Tap the one that changed first. |
| Number Recall | A number flashes up. Type it from memory. First correct answer wins. |
| Pairs | Flip two cards at a time to find matching pairs. Clear all six pairs first. |
| Count Back | Pictures flash one at a time. Count carefully. Then answer how many of one picture you saw. |

### Guessing (6)

| Game | How to win |
| --- | --- |
| Closest Guess | Answer a number question. Closest guess scores a point. First to 2. |
| Dot Count | A cloud of dots flashes for a moment. Guess how many there were. Closest wins the point. First to 2. |
| How Full? | A glass flashes up partly full. Guess how full it was, from 0 to 100%. Closest wins. First to 2. |
| Stop the Clock | A timer starts, then disappears. Tap once to stop yours as close to the target as you can. |
| Higher or Lower | Guess in secret whether the next card is higher or lower. Every right call scores. First to 3. |
| Emoji Movie | Name the movie from the emoji. First right answer scores. First to 3. |

### Bluff & mind games (5)

| Game | How to win |
| --- | --- |
| Rock Paper Scissors | Pick in secret, cover your screen, then reveal. First to 3 wins. Read your opponent. |
| Odds & Evens | One of you is Odds, the other Evens. Both secretly throw 1 or 2. If the total is odd, Odds scores. First to 3. |
| Undercut | Secretly pick 1 to 5. Higher number wins, unless the other player picked exactly one less. Then they undercut you. First to 3. |
| Sealed Bid | You each have 10 chips. Secretly bid for 3 prizes. Highest bid takes the prize. Chips you bid are gone. Win 2 prizes. |
| Bust | Both hold to start. A multiplier climbs. Let go to bank it. It can crash any moment, and anyone still holding gets nothing. Highest bank wins. |

### Brain (5)

| Game | How to win |
| --- | --- |
| True or False | Sums flash up. Tap only when the sum is TRUE. Tap a false one and you lose. |
| Trivia Showdown | Quick-fire questions. First right answer scores. A wrong answer locks you out of that question. First to 3. |
| Scramble | Unscramble the word and tap the right answer first. First to 3. |
| Next in Line | Spot the pattern and tap the number that comes next. First to 3. |
| Which Is Bigger? | Two sums appear. Tap the bigger one. Wrong taps cost a point. First to 5. |

### Words & drawing (4)

| Game | How to win |
| --- | --- |
| Categories | Take turns naming something in the category. Tap after you say it. Run out of time and you lose. No repeats. |
| Rhyme Time | Take turns saying a word that rhymes. Tap after you say it. Run out of time and you lose. No repeats. |
| Word Chain | Each word must start with the last letter of the one before. Take turns. Run out of time and you lose. |
| Quick Sketch | You both get 25 seconds to draw the same thing on your side. Then the crowd votes for the best drawing. |

### Real-life challenges (crowd judges) (12)

| Game | How to win |
| --- | --- |
| Staring Contest | Lock eyes. First to blink, look away or laugh loses. The crowd watches closely. |
| Thumb War | Lock fingers, thumbs up. "One, two, three, four, I declare a thumb war." Pin their thumb for a three-count. Best of 3. |
| Poker Face | Take turns: 30 seconds each to make your opponent laugh or smile. No touching. Whoever cracks first loses. If nobody cracks, the crowd decides. |
| Tongue Twister | Each of you says this three times, fast. Cleanest and quickest wins. The crowd decides. |
| Flamingo | Stand on one leg, arms crossed, eyes closed. First to put a foot down or open their eyes loses. |
| Wall Sit | Backs flat against a wall, knees bent at 90 degrees. First to stand up or slide down loses. |
| Arm Wrestle | Elbows on the table, hands locked. The ref says go. First to pin the other's hand wins. |
| Paper Toss | Crumple a napkin or paper. Take turns tossing it into a cup or bin from three big steps away. Five shots each. Most baskets wins. |
| Hand Slap | One player holds hands palms up; the other rests theirs palms down on top. The bottom player tries to slap; the top player dodges. Swap after a miss. First to 3 slaps. |
| Impressions | Each of you does this impression for 15 seconds. The crowd picks the best one. |
| Hum That Tune | Each player peeks at a secret song, then hums it. Whoever gets their song guessed by the crowd first wins. |
| No-Um Speech | 30 seconds each on this topic. Say "um" or "uh", or pause too long, and you're out. If you both survive, best speech wins. |

## Play it full screen (no bars)

1. Turn on GitHub Pages: repo **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Deploy from a branch**, pick the branch with the game and `/ (root)`, then Save.
2. After a minute, open the Pages link (`https://ghurley4444.github.io/The-Berg-Project/`) in **Safari** on iPhone, or Chrome on Android.
3. iPhone: tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Android: tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or use the Full screen button in the menu).
4. Open the game from the new home-screen icon. It runs full screen with no browser or Claude bars.

Tournament progress is saved on that phone, so close and reopen any time.

## Run it locally

No build step and no dependencies. Serve the folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open `http://<your-computer-ip>:8000` on your phone (same Wi-Fi).

Tips: lock screen rotation and turn the volume up.

## Adding content

Trivia, emoji movies, categories, tongue twisters, impressions, speech topics and songs live in `js/data.js`. Add lines there. Each mini-game is one entry in `js/games.js`.
