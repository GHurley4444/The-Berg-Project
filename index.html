The Party Game

A duel tournament for the whole party, on one phone. Two people face off across the phone in quick mini-games. Everyone else bets points on who'll win.

  • 1Add everyone. The app draws a bracket.
  • 2Before each duel, the watchers bet points. Backing the underdog pays more.
  • 3Lay the phone flat between the two duelists. Each owns their half of the screen.
  • 4Win duels to climb the bracket. Bet well to top the points board.
